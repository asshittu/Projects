# Criteo report attribution

The report metrics, table, and chart adapt the Criteo Attribution Modeling for Bidding Dataset. The analysis selects qualifying impression paths and aggregates them by campaign. These report adaptations are shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The license applies to the reports derived from Criteo data.

[Criteo data and terms](https://ailab.criteo.com/criteo-attribution-modeling-bidding-dataset/) · [Paper by Diemert, Meynet, Lefortier, and Galland](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06409) · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
